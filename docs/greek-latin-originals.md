# Christian shelf: Greek and Latin originals

Pass of 19 November 2026. The Christian shelf held the Fathers almost only in
English (CCEL's Ante-Nicene and Nicene libraries). This pass adds **51 works in
their original languages: 30 Greek and 21 Latin, 1,542 chapters, 23,207
passages and about 2.1 million words**, stored locally like other licensed
texts.

## 1. Source and rights

- **Source:** Open Greek and Latin and the Perseus Digital Library
  (`First1KGreek`, `csel-dev`, `canonical-greekLit`, `canonical-latinLit`
  on GitHub). Registered as `open-greek-and-latin`: approved, licence
  CC BY-SA 4.0, ingestion status import_tested.
- **Licence:** each TEI file must carry a CC BY-SA licence (the canonical
  repositories are covered by their repository licence). Editions are
  recorded with licence `cc-by-sa` and an attribution naming the editor,
  year and repository file.
- **Edition rule:** the transcription licence covers the digital work, not
  the printed edition beneath it. The importer reads the print year from the
  TEI source description and **rejects any edition printed after 1929**. Eight
  texts were dropped on this rule (Loeb volumes of 1931–1953) and entered in the
  acquisition register as rights_blocked, with public-domain editions named
  where one exists.
- Footnotes, apparatus, page headers and editorial sigla are dropped. Each
  edition's note says so.

## 3. How it works

- `ingest/tei.ts`: a small, dependency-free TEI reader. Chapters are the
  top-level textparts; passages are the leaves (sections or paragraphs; verse
  grouped by stanza or runs of ten lines). Long leaves are split by paragraph,
  then by sentence, into "(i/n)" parts. Tested in `tests/tei.test.ts`.
- `scripts/dev/import-ogl.ts` (`bun run import:ogl`): downloads the TEI files
  into `data/imports/cache/ogl/` (`--offline` reuses the cache, `--only=a,b`
  limits the run), checks licence, print year, language and script, and writes
  the work records, text functions, the source, authors and the manifest
  `data/imports/ogl.json`. References are `chapter.passage`.

## 4. Not imported

Rights-blocked (post-1929 print editions): Basil, *Ad adolescentes* (1934);
Jerome, letters (1933); Minucius Felix, *Octavius* (1931); Tertullian,
*Apologeticum* and *De spectaculis* (1931); Prudentius (1949, 1953).
Located but not usable yet: Irenaeus's Greek fragments (OCR merges columns)
and the Latin *Passio Perpetuae* (no chapter divisions).
